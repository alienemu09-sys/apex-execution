Proprietary Strategy

This Adaptive Proprietary Strategy confirms whether restricting entries by certain qualities improves outcomes. Built from first principles, not fit to known chart examples. Applied at entry time only; open positions are never affected mid-trade. Here are the results.

Tested against real instrument value history, 2023–2026, independently re-derived from raw output — not accepted from a single summary.

Tightest Setting: No Benefit
At the tightest setting tested, the strategy made outcomes slightly worse, not better. Unfiltered halt risk was already near zero — filtering mainly removed winning trades with nothing to offset the lost upside.
Wider Settings: Strong, Consistent Help
At wider settings, the strategy cut single-path halt incidence from roughly 4 in 10 tested configurations down to zero, while terminal capital multiple rose 5–7x on average across the configurations tested.
A Real Trade-Off, Not a Free Lunch
Terminal value and peak equity along the way don't always move together. In several configurations, the strategy produced a smoother, safer end result while lowering the highest point the account reached mid-sequence. The two numbers answer different questions — which matters more depends on what's being optimized for.
Research-Stage, Not Adopted

Full Disclosure — A Tested, Rejected Idea

The Idea We Tested and Dropped

An earlier three-part regime filter was built after noticing that two specific historical dates lined up with a pattern our research team noticed. The pattern was real at both dates — confirmed directly against the underlying data, not assumed. But a filter that correctly explains the two examples it was built from proves very little on its own. The actual test is whether it holds up everywhere else.

It didn't. Below is why, in full.

Check 1 — Every Other Signal
Inconclusive
Beyond the two known dates, most other trip events sat on too few trades to draw a real conclusion from — neither a clean confirmation nor a clean rejection.
Check 2 — Missed Warnings
Weak Evidence
Only 3 of 14 other stretches the filter should have caught showed real, comparable-strength warning signs. The rest were indistinguishable from ordinary market noise.
Check 3 — The Decisive One
Added Nothing
The complex two-part trigger this filter was built around added a 0.12 percentage point difference over a single, much simpler trend line taken alone. Effectively zero incremental value.

This filter is permanently retired from production consideration. It is not run, not referenced, and produces no numbers used anywhere else on this page. The Proprietary Strategy shown above was designed differently — from first principles, not fit to known examples — and has held up under the same kind of scrutiny across a far larger dataset.
